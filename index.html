AboutCredits

This site was made out of courtesy, and in the general interest of everyone who files paperwork for the department.

The aim is simple: one unitary place for everything you need, kept as simple as possible and with as many automations as possible. Fewer fields to retype, fewer formatting mistakes, and one click to copy.

Releasev2.4.1November 9, 2026

v2.4.1

SED — Squad Members

  • Squad Members are now ordered by division (SEB, SED, ESD, CSD, then Training Team) and, inside each division, by departmental rank from highest to lowest. People with the same rank are ordered by their divisional rank, then by name. The Deployment Log pickers use the same order.

Releasev2.4.0November 9, 2026

v2.4.0

SED — Squad Members

  • The roster is no longer loaded into your list by itself. Your saved list stays exactly as it is until you press the new Sync roster button, which brings in the whole Special Operations roster. People on the roster replace any copy of them in your list; everyone else you saved is kept.
  • When the roster has been updated, a notice at the top of Squad Members says what changed (added, removed, changed) and a dot appears on the Squad Members menu item. Nothing happens to your list until you press Sync.
  • Before every Sync your whole list is stored, and a Revert last sync button puts it back exactly as it was.

Releasev2.3.9November 9, 2026

v2.3.9

SED — Squad Members

  • Fixed Squad Members not showing the updated list for some people. Anyone saved by hand with the same name and divisional rank as the roster (for example from an imported CSV) now goes back to following the roster, once, on their next visit. People you changed to a different assignment are left alone, and Character Details is not touched.
  • The site now loads fresh copies of its scripts and styles after each update, so an old cached version is no longer served.

Releasev2.3.8November 9, 2026

v2.3.8

SED — Squad Members

  • The Rank is now set from the Divisional Rank. Choosing a divisional rank fills in the departmental rank (you can still change it): Team Leaders are Sergeants, Detail Commanders are Lieutenants, ESD Tactical Medics and Crew Chiefs are Deputy Sheriff (Bonus II), CSD handlers are Deputy Sheriff, Bonus I or Bonus II by level, and SED Team Members, Scouts and Probationary members follow the same pattern.
  • The roster snapshot now only takes each person's name and divisional rank, and works out the rank from it. People already on the roster have been updated to match.

Releasev2.3.7November 9, 2026

v2.3.7

SED — Squad Members

  • Roster snapshot refreshed. Luke Harrison (Commander) is now included under SEB as Area Commander, listed first in the group.
