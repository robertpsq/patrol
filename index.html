Tips

            
    
              
  • 
                Enter your name, badge number, rank, and bureau in Character Details. Matching
                fields in supported forms and generators will prefill automatically.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                Prefilled fields remain editable. Update Character Details to refresh fields that
                still contain their previous autofilled value.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                Warrant dates default to today's date and can be edited. Warrant affidavits show a
                live preview; use Copy to copy formatted text.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                Use the + and − controls to add or remove evidence and other repeatable entries
                where available.
              
    • 
              
  • Use the Reset button to clear a form and restore its default values.
